Calc Speedrun

Mode: Unit 1

This website aims to fulfill the desire to make everyone enrolled in AP Calculus BC get A+ on every tests and acquire a 5 in May. It breaks down how to use Desmos graphing calculator for AP exam, TI84 Calculator for in-class tests, and hopefully Calculus concepts to intuitive level. Calculus may be conceptually easier to understand than Pre-Calculus materials; connecting multiple concepts into one and applying them is what makes it hard for many people. However, every year, around 40% of test takers end up with a 5... Wouldn't it be sorrowful if you fail to enter top 40% in your class? I hope what I created gives some help to people who struggle with math, especially for those who struggle even under the best math teacher ever, Mr O...
Your day will come, and you shall find yourself in possession of 5 in July.
Made by Jay Jeong(438668@aaps.k12.mi.us).
