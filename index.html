BC Quick Sheet

01

Derivatives

  • Power, product, quotient, chain, implicit, inverse, and logarithmic differentiation.

  • Parametric slope and concavity come from differentiating with respect to the parameter.

  • Polar derivatives combine radius and angle change.

02

Integrals

  • Definite integrals measure accumulation, signed area, average value, and net change.

  • Use substitution, parts, partial fractions, and calculator-supported numerical setup.

  • Polar area and volume problems depend on a clean setup before evaluation.

03

Series

  • Geometric, p-series, comparison, limit comparison, ratio, and alternating tests.

  • Find radius and interval, then check endpoints separately.

  • Taylor polynomials require center, degree, coefficient pattern, and error reasoning.

04

Exam Moves

  • State variables, include units, and answer in the context of the problem.

  • For extrema and intervals, use a sign chart or a derivative justification.

  • Write the mathematical setup before touching the calculator.
